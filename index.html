From the Studio

Twelve years at the wheel, one kiln at a time.

I started throwing pots in a community studio in 2012 and never really stopped. These days the work happens in a converted garage off Kiln Street, where every piece is thrown, trimmed, glazed, and fired by hand before it reaches a shelf.

No two glaze runs come out quite the same, which is the part I like most.
